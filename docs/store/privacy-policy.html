ScaleSmith Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE OF PUBLICATION]

ScaleSmith is a practice app for guitar, bass, ukulele and other string instruments. This policy explains what the app does with information.

In short: ScaleSmith does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

Who is responsible

ScaleSmith is made by [YOUR NAME], an independent developer based in Finland. Contact: scalesmith.app@gmail.com

Microphone

The tuner and the slide intonation trainer use your device's microphone to hear the note you play.

  • The microphone is used only while one of these screens is open and listening. It stops when you stop listening or leave the screen.
  • The sound is analysed on your device, in real time, to find its pitch.
  • The sound is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

You can deny or remove the microphone permission at any time in your device settings. The rest of the app works without it.

Information saved on your device

To remember your choices, the app saves these on your device only:

  • your instrument, tuning, theme, fretboard wood and note colours
  • scales you create yourself
  • practice results and records in the ear training exercises and the neck game
  • other small preferences, such as the tuner's reference pitch

This information never leaves your device through the app. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your device settings.

No accounts, ads or tracking

  • The app has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email or other contact details.
  • The app shows no advertising.
  • The app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising software, and it does not send information to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

If you buy ScaleSmith Premium, the purchase is handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether Premium has been bought on your account. Google's privacy policy applies to purchases: policies.google.com/privacy

Google Play

Google may collect information when you download the app or use Google Play, for example to provide crash reports to developers if you have allowed that in your device settings. This is governed by Google's privacy policy, not by this one.

Children

ScaleSmith collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
