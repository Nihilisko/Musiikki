## Full description (max 4000)

ScaleSmith is a practice workshop for guitar, bass, ukulele, 12-string, cigar box and slide guitar. Pick your instrument and tuning once, and everything in the app follows it: scales, chords, shapes and exercises are worked out for the strings you actually play.

SCALES AND ARPEGGIOS
• 13 scales and modes in every key, from major and minor to Dorian, Mixolydian, harmonic minor and the blues scales
• Note names spelled correctly for the key (B♭ in F major, not A♯)
• Show note names, degrees or both on a fretboard coloured the way you like
• Blue notes, pentatonic box positions and 3-notes-per-string positions
• Arpeggios, the circle of fifths and chord progressions drawn on the neck
• Build and save your own scales

CHORDS
• A chord library that works out playable shapes for any instrument and tuning
• Triads and inversions on every string set
• Open tuning maps for slide and cigar box guitar
• CAGED shapes with the scale around each one

THEORY
• Capo and transpose calculator that finds the key of a song and the easiest capo position
• The chords of every major and minor key, with their functions
• Short, clear theory notes on intervals, scales, modes and chords

BACKING TRACKS
• A drone on any note, to practise scales and intonation against
• Chord progressions with drums, piano and bass: rock, blues shuffle, pop and jazz swing
• Bass lines on a bass neck for bass players, strum patterns for everyone else
• Any key, any tempo

EAR TRAINING
• Intervals, chord types, scale degrees and chord progressions
• Five levels in each exercise, or pick exactly what to practise
• See each answer on your own instrument

TUNER AND METRONOME
• A vintage-style tuner that recognises the string you play, with a chime when it is in tune
• Adjustable reference pitch (432–446 Hz)
• Metronome with time signatures, accents, subdivisions, tap tempo and a speed trainer

GAMES AND TRAINERS (PREMIUM)
• Neck game: find notes, degrees and chord tones on your neck, with a one-minute challenge and records
• Slide intonation trainer: play target notes with the slide and hold them in tune
• CAGED system view

Premium is a one-time purchase. There are no subscriptions, no ads and no account to create.

PRIVACY
The tuner and slide trainer listen through the microphone only while they are open. The sound is processed on your device and is never recorded or sent anywhere. ScaleSmith collects no personal data.
